How to Host Your Portfolio

Because this is a high-performance static site, public visitors see whatever is saved in your index.html file. Follow these simple steps:

Step 1: Make your edits Click "Edit portfolio" in the navbar, update your profile info, portrait picture, or add projects.
Step 2: Click "Publish Live HTML" This downloads an updated index.html file with all your changes and projects embedded permanently.
Step 3: Upload to free hosting • GitHub Pages: Push or upload index.html to your GitHub repo, go to Settings > Pages and choose Deploy from branch.
• Netlify: Drag and drop the folder with index.html into netlify.com/drop.
• Vercel: Connect your GitHub repo for automatic continuous deploys.
